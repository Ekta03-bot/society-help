# Problem Statement

Residential societies in India commonly rely on domestic helpers who may work across multiple households.

Most societies primarily track whether a domestic helper enters or exits the society premises. However, there may be limited or no centralized mechanism to track which flats the helper is assigned to, whether the helper actually visited those flats on a particular day, or whether the helper was absent.

For example, a domestic helper may be assigned to three flats but may visit only one of them on a particular day. A society-level entry record alone cannot identify this.

This creates gaps in both security monitoring and household-level attendance tracking.

The goal of SocietyHelp is to create a centralized system that manages domestic-helper identity, flat assignments, gate entry and exit records, and eventually flat-level attendance.
